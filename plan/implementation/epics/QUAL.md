Conventions: "§" references are to the RFC named in the task's Implements column. Milestones follow RFC-0001 §16 (M0–M4). Every number in an acceptance criterion is a target from `00-vision-and-scope.md` §7 (cited as SC-n) or from the named RFC's own budget tables. Cross-area dependencies use the prefixes PLC, TIER, DIST, COMP and PROD. Where the other area's task ID is not yet known, the dependency is described in words for the integrator to resolve.

M0 critical path for this area: E1 (workspace and CI skeleton) → E3 (determinism types, fakes, guards) → E4 and E7 (reference executor, nano fixtures) → E5 (parity harness and gate) → E12 (benchmark harness on R-ADA). That chain produces RFC-0001's M0 exit evidence: parity passes for one dense and one MoE family, and SC-4 holds for the all-`Vram` case on the CUDA rig.

## Epic QUAL-E1: Workspace, CI skeleton and policy checks
- Goal: from the first merge, every PR builds the workspace on every Tier 1 OS, runs the hosted suites, and is checked against the dependency, licence and contribution rules. This holds within the hosted budget, with one required check and no hardware time spent on irrelevant changes.
- RFC sections implemented: RFC-0062 §1, §2 (2.1–2.5), §8.3, §11.1, §11.2, §11.4 (new direct dependencies), §12 (12.1–12.6), §13, §14, "Performance model and budgets", "Testing and validation" (items 1–3, 11, 13–15); RFC-0060 §1.2
- ADRs implemented: ADR-0620, ADR-0621 (decisions 2, 3), ADR-0624, ADR-0021 (decisions 2, 4, 5; revisit trigger), ADR-0018 (§1–§3, §5, §6), ADR-0001 (stable toolchain pin, `unsafe` policy), ADR-0012 (§6 CI invariants), ADR-0019 (§9 trigger paths), ADR-0020 (path-filtered hardware jobs)
- Crates touched: workspace root, `xtask`, every crate's manifest (stubs), `.github/`, `ci/`

## Epic QUAL-E17: Versioning, release pipeline, compatibility and dependencies
- Goal: one product version driven by conventional commits. Releases are gated on the exact signed bits and move from beta to stable without rebuilding. A tested compatibility policy lets home clusters upgrade one machine at a time. Numerics-critical dependencies, ggml first, never change without a full conformance run.
- RFC sections implemented: RFC-0062 §8 (8.1–8.2), §9 (9.1–9.3), §10 (10.1–10.4), §11.3, §11.4, "Testing and validation" item 7, Open questions 3, 6–9

- Crates touched: `xtask` (`release`, `vendor`), `core` (protocol-version constants), `third_party/ggml/`, `.github/workflows/release*.yml`
