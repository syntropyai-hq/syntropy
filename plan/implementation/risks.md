# Risk register

Sources, as of 2026-10-20:
- `plan/open-questions.md`: the open questions. Only IDs that are still open are cited as open; a settled ID is cited with the ADR or RFC section that settled it.
- The RFCs and ADRs as they stand on 2026-10-20, including RFC-0001 §18's amendment log.
- The platform limits the plan accepts by design (RFC-0011 §15, RFC-0020 §16, RFC-0012 §7.5, RFC-0022 §5.3, ADR-0020).
- The generated integration files: `roadmap.md` (critical paths, gating decisions, open questions without a task) and `dependencies.md` (graph checks, external gates).
- ADR-0021, the development model: AI agents build Syntropy, gates and an independent review agent review every change, and maintainers are the only human role (ADR-0000 D8).

**What changed since the previous register.** The specifications have no known open gaps: every problem found in them has been fixed in the RFC or ADR concerned. The task graph has no cycles, no later-milestone dependencies and no M0-closure problems (`dependencies.md`). What remains is mostly unmeasured: assumptions that only the lab can test, and limits the plan accepts on some platforms. A fix in an RFC lowers a risk's likelihood but does not remove it, because nothing has run it yet.

ADR-0021 sets how Syntropy is built, and the register follows it. Capacity is limited by rig time and gate throughput, so R03 covers the rigs, and R38, R39 and R42 cover escaped defects, prompt injection and drift across many changes. The register holds technical and delivery risks only.

**Changes on 2026-10-20.** Rows state the designs settled on 2026-10-20 rather than decisions pending. R33 is retired because every question it listed now has a task, and the governance risks R34, R40, R41 and R43 are retired because this register covers technical and delivery risks only.

**Ratings.** Likelihood and impact are qualitative judgements, not measured probabilities.
- Likelihood **High**: the condition holds now, either because the plan accepts it by design or because the gap is in the plan today. **Medium**: a decision is outstanding, or the design depends on an assumption nothing has measured, or a fix has not been exercised. **Low**: the fix is specified, and a fallback exists if the implementation falls short.
- Impact **High**: a milestone exit or an SC target fails. **Medium**: an SC target degrades, a work stream stalls, or a Tier 1 platform loses a capability users will notice. **Low**: local rework, or a configuration no SC gates.
- **Rank** orders the risks by impact, then likelihood, then how early the affected milestone is. IDs carry over from the previous register where the risk does; new risks started at R31, and those added for ADR-0021 start at R38. Retired IDs are listed at the end and are not reused.

**Owner** names the area whose tasks carry the mitigation, or "maintainers" where the mitigation needs a Maintainer action. Areas are domains, not teams (ADR-0021 decision 3). Figures marked [estimate] come from an RFC or the research and have not been measured by Syntropy.
