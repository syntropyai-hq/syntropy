# Open questions

This register lists the technical questions the Syntropy plan still leaves open, and the answers to the ones it has settled. It collects RFC-0001 §17, the "Open questions" sections of the 24 domain RFCs, the measurements that ADR revisit clauses still wait on, and the spike and decision tasks in `plan/implementation/epics/`. 143 questions are open. Answered questions are in [Settled](#settled) at the end, each with the ADR or RFC section that holds the answer.

How to read it:
- **RFC** is the RFC that records the answer. Where two are listed, the first one holds it.
- **Resolution** is how the question closes:
  - *spike*: a time-boxed prototype or probe on real hardware, ending in a recorded decision
  - *benchmark*: a repeatable measurement under RFC-0061, stored with the suite
  - *decision*: a call recorded in that RFC, from the evidence named. The agent executing the task makes it, and the gates and the review agent check it; changes that ADR-0021 reserves also need a maintainer's approval
  - *check*: reading a published artefact, such as a checkpoint's configuration or reference code
- **Task** is the epic task whose exit records the answer. "—" means no task carries it yet.
- **Needed by** is the milestone that cannot exit without the answer: M0a walking skeleton, M0b parity, certification and the lab, M1a Linux preview (CUDA and CPU tiering), M1b Windows and Apple silicon, M2 multi-device on LAN, M3 feature complete, M4 hardening and 1.0. "post-1.0" marks questions the RFCs defer past v1.
- "RFC-0011 OQ2" means item 2 of RFC-0011's Open questions section.
- Figures marked [estimate] come from the research or an RFC and have not been measured by Syntropy.

When a question closes, move it to Settled with the ADR or RFC section that answers it. Do not delete it, and never reuse an ID.
